Barka da zuwa AInotes
====================
AInotes yana mayar da rubutun hannu, hotunan allo, takardun PDF da fayilolin (`.txt`, `.md`, `.mdx`) zuwa tsararrun bayanan karatu na dijital ga daliban kimiyyar kwamfuta.

Gane Rubutun Hannu da Daukar Hoto
---------------------------------
1. **Allon Alkalami (Stylus Canvas)**: Rubuta algorithms kai tsaye akan allo sannan ka mayar da su rubutun dijital da lambar shirye-shirye.
2. **Na'urar Daukar Hoto da PDF**: Loda hotunan rubutun hannu ta amfani da tsarin MyScript, Pen to Print, Transkribus, Document AI da Textract.
3. **Shigo da Fayiloli (`.txt`, `.md`, `.mdx`)**: Loda ko liƙa takardun rubutu kai tsaye cikin bayanan ka.

   def binary_search(arr, target):
       low, high = 0, len(arr) - 1
       while low <= high:
           mid = (low + high) // 2
           if arr[mid] == target:
               return mid
           elif arr[mid] < target:
               low = mid + 1
           else:
               high = mid - 1
       return -1
